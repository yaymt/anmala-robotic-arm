\# Troubleshooting



During the Anmala V1 build, several issues were encountered

during the printing, electronics, power, and assembly stages.



\## 3D Printing Issues



Some printed parts failed during printing. In one case, a part

detached from the print surface during the printing process and

had to be printed again.



\## Printer Software



The printer's original software/firmware setup caused some

issues during the project. The printer was therefore configured

to use \*\*Klipper\*\* as an alternative firmware environment.



\## Power System



The initial buck converter used in the power system did not

operate as expected. It was replaced with a different buck

converter that was suitable for the required power setup.



\## Servo Driver



The servo driver presented power/current limitations during

operation, particularly when the servos were operating at

higher speeds. This required changes to the power and control

setup.



\## Mechanical Assembly



Some challenges were encountered during the assembly of the

original mechanical design. In particular, routing and managing

the wires through certain areas of the structure was difficult.



Additional adjustments were required during assembly to ensure

that the components and wiring could be properly installed.



\## General Troubleshooting



The build required several practical adjustments during

assembly and testing to achieve reliable operation of the

mechanical, electrical, and control systems.
