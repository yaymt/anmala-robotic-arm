**# Calibration**



**Calibration was performed to ensure that the robotic arm joints**

**and gripper operated within their intended movement ranges.**



**The calibration process included:**



**- Setting the initial position of the servos.**

**- Adjusting the potentiometer control ranges.**

**- Checking the movement range of each joint.**

**- Adjusting servo positions to reduce unwanted movement.**

**- Testing the gripper opening and closing positions.**



**The calibration was performed after the mechanical and electrical**

**assembly to ensure that the arm could operate correctly before**

**final testing.**
