\# Testing



After completing the assembly and calibration, the Anmala V1

robotic arm was tested to verify the operation of its main

components.



\## Tests Performed



\- Servo movement test

\- Joint movement test

\- Gripper opening and closing test

\- Potentiometer control test

\- Overall system operation test



\## Results



The tests were used to verify the movement and response of the

robotic arm and to identify any remaining issues requiring

adjustment.



Further testing and improvements may be performed in future

versions of Anmala.
