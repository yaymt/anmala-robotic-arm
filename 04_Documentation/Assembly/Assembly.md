\# Assembly



The Anmala V1 robotic arm was assembled after completing the

3D printing of the required mechanical components.



The assembly process included:



\- Installing the printed mechanical components.

\- Installing and securing the servo motors.

\- Installing the gripper mechanism and gears.

\- Connecting the electronic components.

\- Connecting the power system.

\- Installing the control components.

\- Checking the movement of the joints after assembly.



The printed components were assembled according to the original

design and adapted to the available hardware used in this build.



\## Final Assembly



After completing the mechanical and electrical assembly, the

robotic arm was tested to verify the movement of its joints and

the operation of the gripper.
