# 3D Printing

The robotic arm components were manufactured using 3D printing
and assembled as part of the Anmala V1 build.

## Printer

- **3D Printer:** Creality Ender-3 S1 Pro
- **Nozzle:** 0.4 mm

## Materials

Both PLA and PETG filament were used during the build.

The two materials were mainly used to provide different colors
and visually distinguish between the printed components, based
on the materials available during the project.

## Printing Process

The parts were printed individually and assembled after
successful completion of the prints.

Custom slicer settings were used and adjusted during the printing
process according to the requirements of the parts.

## Printing Challenges

Some parts experienced printing failures during the build.
For example, a part detached from the print surface during
printing and had to be printed again.

Another challenge was related to the printer's original
firmware/software environment. This led to a change in the
printer software and the adoption of **Klipper** to improve
the printing setup and continue the project.

## Notes

The final printed components were selected from the successful
prints and used for the assembly of Anmala V1.
